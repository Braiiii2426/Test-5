My Confession

My Heart had already chosen you without me even realizing it.

♥

I’m not really sure when it happened, but somewhere between our conversations and the little moments we shared, I slowly started to like you. It wasn’t something I planned or expected. It just happened quietly, little by little, until I realized that I was already looking forward to seeing you, talking to you, and simply being around you.

There’s something about you that I find genuinely beautiful. Not only because you’re adorable, but because of the way you carry yourself. I admire your sympathy, your kindness, and how true you are to yourself. I’ve heard that you tend to avoid things that might hurt someone, especially when it comes to words or certain topics. That small part of you made me admire you even more, because it shows how much you care about the feelings of others.

You’re also the quiet and shy kind of person, at least from what I’ve noticed. And somehow, I find that really charming. You don’t need to say much to catch my attention. Sometimes, it’s simply the way you talk, the way you smile, or the little expressions you make. Your cute outfits, your quiet intelligence, and the way you’re friendly with people—all of those little things somehow make you, you. And I think that’s what I like most.

Being your friend has been one of the reasons these feelings grew naturally. We get along, our conversations feel comfortable, and I enjoy the simple moments we share. I never really noticed how much those moments meant to me until I started realizing that I was slowly becoming attached to them. Somehow, the person I was simply happy to call a friend became someone I began looking at a little differently.

I’ll be honest, though—I was afraid to tell you. I kept thinking about what might happen if you didn’t feel the same way. Maybe I was afraid of rejection, or maybe I was just scared that saying these things would make our friendship different. So I stayed quiet for a while, keeping these feelings somewhere in the back of my mind. But I realized that sometimes, being honest is better than wondering what could have happened if I had only found the courage to say it.

So, here I am, finally saying it. I like you. And I think I’m slowly, quietly, and genuinely falling for you. I’m not asking you for anything, and you don’t have to feel the same way. I just wanted you to know what has been in my heart for a while. Whatever your answer may be, I’ll still be grateful that I got to know you, become your friend, and discover all those little things about you that made me feel this way in the first place.

Some words are easier to write than to say. So here they are, quietly waiting for you.

With all the sincerity my heart can offer.
